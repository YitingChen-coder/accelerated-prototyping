01 / THE METHOD

Stop solving alone.
Start explaining.

You know the algorithm. But can you explain why it works while someone asks a follow-up? That’s the part a problem set rarely rehearses.

Rehearse is an AI-powered SWE mock interview coach. Practice realistic coding, system design, and behavioral interviews through a conversation that makes room for your thinking.

Then get personalized feedback on your reasoning, solution, and communication. Choose one gap, practice it again, and arrive at your next interview with a little more clarity.

EDITOR’S NOTE

Practice isn’t about sounding perfect. It’s about knowing what to improve.
